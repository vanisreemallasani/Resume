The Best Movies According to Vanisree

My Top 3 Movies of All Time

1.Hii Nana

Hi Nanna is one of my favourite movie. I really like the emotional bond between the father and daughter. The story and characters make this movie special to me.

2.Ala Vaikuntapuramloo

I really enjoyed this movie because of its entertaining story,family relationships, music and comedy. It is a movie I can watch and enjoy again.

3.Maharshi

I like Maharshi because of its inspiring story. I especially enjoyed the hero's journey,friendship and the message about the importance of helping others.
